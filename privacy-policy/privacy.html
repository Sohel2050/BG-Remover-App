1. What the App does with your photos

Background removal, brush editing, filters, and all other image processing happen entirely on your device. Your photos are not uploaded to our servers — we don't operate any server that receives your images, because none of the app's editing features need one.

Processed images are stored in the app's local storage on your device (for the Recent Projects feature) until you delete them from the app or uninstall it. Images you choose to save go to your device's photo gallery; images you choose to share go wherever you direct them (e.g. a messaging app) via your device's native share sheet.

3. Advertising

This App is free and supported by ads, served through Unity LevelPlay, which mediates ads from multiple ad networks. These networks may collect data such as your advertising ID, device information, and general location (derived from IP address) to show and measure ads, and some may use this data for personalized advertising depending on your region's settings and your choices (e.g. iOS App Tracking Transparency, Android ad personalization settings).

4. App update checks

On launch, the App checks a small configuration file hosted on GitHub to see if a newer version is available or required. This request does not include your photos or any personal information — only a standard network request to fetch a public file.

5. Data we do NOT collect

We do not operate analytics or crash-reporting servers of our own, we do not require account creation or login, and we do not sell your data. (Third-party ad networks listed above operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies linked above.)
